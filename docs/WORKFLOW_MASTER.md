## Overview

This document defines the **7-Phase Architectural Design Workflow** used by TruPlans Inc. for all residential design projects. It serves as:

1. **Design Manual** — Standard operating procedure for all designers
2. **Dashboard Reference** — Data model for TruPlans Dashboard (Supabase + React/Electron)
3. **Agent Framework Foundation** — Blueprint for 7-agent architecture (each phase = 1 autonomous agent)

**Total Timeline:** \~120 business days (4–5 months)  
**Total Phases:** 7  
**Total Steps:** 24+  
**Approval Gates:** 7

\---

## Phase 1: PROJECT KICKOFF

**Duration:** 2–3 business days  
**Owner:** Lead Designer + Marketing (Ricardo)  
**Trigger:** Contract signed

### Deliverables

1. **Contract Handover** — Signed agreement reviewed; scope extracted; welcome email sent; site scheduling confirmed
2. **Materiality \& Measurement** — On-site survey completed; all measurements documented; 3D scan acquired (interior \& exterior 100%)
3. **Drawing Existing Plan** — Existing conditions drafted in CAD/Chief Architect
4. **Zoning \& HOA Research** — All zoning, setback, and HOA requirements documented
5. **Layout Meeting with Client** — Client vision discussed; ideas collected; notes compiled
6. **Floor System Verification** — Contractors verify structural capacity; doors, windows, setbacks, floor thickness documented
7. **Office Pre-Scope Meeting** — Proposed solution drafted with Chris direction

### Key Activities

* Prepare site measurement kit (layout, laser, pens, colors)
* Study measurement templates (standardized notation)
* Collect construction documentation on-site
* Request HOA association information + client authorization
* Email city/county for setback lines \& property lines
* Coordinate 3D scanning (100% interior + 100% exterior coverage)

### Approval Gate

✅ **GATE:** Contract signed + site access confirmed + all Phase 1 research complete

**→ Proceed to Phase 2**

\---

## Phase 2: DESIGN DEVELOPMENTS

**Duration:** 7–14 business days  
**Owner:** Lead Designer + Design Team  
**Trigger:** Phase 1 complete

### Deliverables

1. **Existing Plan Preparation** — Existing plan drawn in CAD/Chief Architect
2. **Proposed Plan \& 3D Model** — Proposed design created; 3D renderings generated; design meetings scheduled
3. **Plan Revision** — Client feedback incorporated; revised plans prepared (typically 1 week)
4. **Critical Moment of Change (C.H.O.C.)** — Addendums documented; Lorena prepares contract amendments; client signature obtained

### Key Activities

* Create 3D clips/videos showing final design
* Schedule design meeting (Zoom)
* Meet with Chris for meeting preparation \& strategy
* Present design to client; collect feedback
* (Optional) Conduct additional design meeting if needed
* Prepare detailed plans with all revisions
* Send plans for electronic signature

### Payment Checkpoint

💰 **Track contract payment milestones** — When client pays, designer receives payment

### Approval Gate

✅ **GATE:** Client approves design \& electronically signs plans (Design Hold sign-off)

**→ Proceed to Phase 3**

\---

## Phase 3: CONSTRUCTION DOCUMENTS — SET 1 (ARCHITECTURAL)

**Duration:** 5–7 business days  
**Owner:** Lead Designer + Specs Team  
**Trigger:** Plan Revision approved

### Deliverables

1. **Collect All Architectural Components** — Gather plans, sections, elevations, details, calculations, door/window schedules
2. **Prepare Architectural Details \& Electrical** — Create construction documents as coloring sheets; prepare wall sections, door details; create power \& lighting plans (1–2 sheets)

### Key Activities

* Organize all architectural components per TruPlans standards
* QA check (Calibers!) for missing dimensions, errors
* Prepare electrical power plan
* Prepare electrical lighting plan
* Package for HOA submission

### Approval Gate

✅ **GATE:** CD Set 1 locked + HOA submission package ready

**→ Proceed to Phase 4**

\---

## Phase 4: HOA SUBMISSIONS \& APPROVALS

**Duration:** 10–30 business days (max 45 days for approval)  
**Owner:** Lead Designer  
**Trigger:** CD Set 1 ready

### Deliverables

1. **HOA Submittal Preparation** — HOA cover letter; neighbor approval letters; research HOA design guidelines; submit complete package
2. **HOA Corrections** (if applicable) — Receive corrections; revise drawings; resubmit corrected plans
3. **HOA Approval** — Final approval received from HOA association

### Key Activities

* Gather HOA information from association records
* Create professional cover letter
* Collect neighbor signatures (proof of awareness)
* Research HOA-specific zoning/planning requirements
* Submit initial package
* Respond to any HOA comments within 7–10 days
* Resubmit if corrections needed

### ⚠️ CRITICAL

**Discover ALL HOA rules in Phase 1** to avoid delays in Phase 4. Timeline issues = project delays.

### Approval Gate

✅ **GATE:** HOA approval received

**→ Proceed to Phase 5**

\---

## Phase 5: CONSTRUCTION DOCUMENTS — SET 2 (STRUCTURAL \& ENGINEERING)

**Duration:** 7–14 business days  
**Owner:** Lead Designer + Structural Engineer + MEP  
**Trigger:** HOA approval + CD Set 1 locked

### Deliverables

1. **Contractor Verification \& Consultant Coordination** — Verify all construction elements with contractors; email Structural, MEP, \& Title 24 engineers; coordinate all systems
2. **Finalization of Construction Documents** — Gather all consultant deliverables; perform final inspection; QA check; notify client

### Key Consultants

* **Structural Engineer** — Calculations + sealed structural drawings
* **MEP Engineer** — Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing systems
* **Title 24 Engineer** — Energy code compliance
* **3D Scanning Specialist** — (completed in Phase 1)

### Key Activities

* Reference Phase 1 contractor verifications
* Email consultants with full construction details
* Collect green sheets (color-coded coordination sheets)
* Perform designer-to-designer QA review
* Ensure all consultant sign-offs obtained
* Email client: "Construction package ready"

### Approval Gate

✅ **GATE:** Structural package sealed + all consultant sign-offs received

**→ Proceed to Phase 6**

\---

## Phase 6: PERMITTING (CITY/COUNTY SUBMISSIONS \& APPROVALS)

**Duration:** 21–60+ business days  
**Owner:** Lead Designer + Permitting Coordinator  
**Trigger:** Structural package locked

### Deliverables

1. **Find Submission Portals** — Identify all city/county upload portals; confirm requirements
2. **Upload to Portal** — Upload all plans to CT or county portal; notify client of fees
3. **Submittal of Package** — Official submittal; receive permit number 1
4. **City/County Review** — Planning \& Building review (up to 30 days)
5. **Resubmittals** (if needed) — Respond to comments (7–10 days); resubmit (may have 1–3 cycles)
6. **Permit Fees \& Issuance** — Client pays fees; building permit issued
7. **Permit Paperwork Preparation** — Organize stamped documents; prepare for Phase 7

### Key Activities

* Research city/county submission portals
* Upload complete construction document package
* Track city review timeline
* Address any planning comments promptly
* Request permit issuance once approved
* Organize all stamped \& approved sheets
* Prepare final permit package for contractors

**Note:** Ask Lorena for assistance if portal issues arise.

### Approval Gate

✅ **GATE:** Permit issued + all paperwork prepared

**→ Proceed to Phase 7**

\---

## Phase 7: PRE-CONSTRUCTION

**Duration:** 2–5 business days  
**Owner:** Lead Designer + Design Team + Contractor  
**Trigger:** Permit issued

### Deliverables

1. **Handoff Meeting with Contractor** — Meet contractor (without homeowner); review all plans \& documentation
2. **Pre-Construction Meeting** — Meet with contractor, client, \& design team; present final design intent; official project handoff to field construction

### Key Activities

* Schedule handoff meeting with contractor
* Review all construction documents with contractor
* Discuss construction phasing, timing, logistics
* Answer contractor questions
* Schedule pre-construction meeting with all parties
* Finalize any last-minute coordination
* Official handoff: Project moves to construction

### Approval Gate

✅ **GATE:** Pre-construction meeting held — project moves to construction phase

**→ CONSTRUCTION BEGINS**

## Approval Gates (Sequential)

1. **Phase 1 → Phase 2:** Contract signed + site access confirmed + all research complete
2. **Phase 2 → Phase 3:** Client approves design \& electronically signs plans (Design Hold sign-off)
3. **Phase 3 → Phase 4:** CD Set 1 locked + HOA submission package ready
4. **Phase 4 → Phase 5:** HOA approval received
5. **Phase 5 → Phase 6:** Structural package sealed + all consultant sign-offs received
6. **Phase 6 → Phase 7:** Permit issued + all paperwork prepared
7. **Phase 7 → Handoff:** Pre-construction meeting held — ready for construction

## Quality Standards

### Calibers! (Phase 3)

* All drawings QA checked
* No missing dimensions
* No conflicting notes
* Ready for HOA submission

### C.H.O.C. (Phase 2)

* Critical height changes flagged
* All addendums documented
* Client aware of cost impact
* Signature confirmation obtained

### Contractor Verification (Phase 1 \& 5)

* Structural capacity confirmed
* All measurements verified
* 3D scan coverage 100% (interior + exterior)

### City Plan Review (Phase 6)

* Submit clean package on first round when possible
* Track city timelines
* Respond to comments within 7–10 days
